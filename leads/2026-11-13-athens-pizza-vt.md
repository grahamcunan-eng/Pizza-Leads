- **Instagram:** none found
- **Online ordering:** https://athenspizza.hungerrush.com
- **Rating:** 7/10

## Problems found
- No menu page on the site; the navigation is only Home, Story, Gallery, Order Online and Apply Now, so the menu is only visible through the ordering system.
- Stale pandemic-era copy on the homepage: "Our restaurant is now open for dining in!"
- Odd leftover instructions about ordering "from a Dorm," and a capitalized warning that online orders won't work on a VPN, take up the main homepage space.
- A scrolling "now hiring" banner repeats three times across the homepage.
- No social links on the site.

## Why it's a good opportunity
Squarespace site that's serviceable but vague and out of date, with the most useful content (menu, hours) missing from the site itself.

## Design notes
Add a real menu page and hours, rewrite the homepage around the family restaurant, move the ordering warnings to the checkout, and link the Facebook page.
