# Papa Jay's Pizzeria (Dover, NH) - 9/10
- Website: https://www.papajays.net/
- Location: Downtown Dover, NH (between Red Shoe Barn and the Dover Fire Station, next to the railroad trestle, per the site)
- Phone: (603) 750-7272
- Rating: 9/10
## Problems found
- Homepage slider still shows the unedited Drupal theme demo slides: "Drupal 8 Theme", "Zymphonies Theme" and "Drupal Design & Development", each with Lorem Ipsum dummy text and "Read more" buttons
- "Contact Us" and "Employment" pages are empty, showing only a heading ("Contact Us", "HIRING") with no form, address or details
- "Our Location" page is just a heading and an embedded Google Map; no street address written anywhere on the site
- No menu on the site at all (/menu returns "Page not found"); the menu only exists on third-party ordering sites
- Two different "Order Now" destinations (weborder.net and Slice), which is confusing
- Hours table reads "9:30-11:00" with no AM/PM, plus an empty row
- Plain stacked-text homepage with very little visual design
## Why it's a good opportunity
A locally owned downtown shop open since 2001 whose site is a POS-vendor Drupal template with demo Lorem Ipsum slides still live, empty pages and no menu, so a clean rebuild is an obvious, easy-to-show upgrade.
## Design notes
Use the shop's existing content only. Never use AI-generated food images. Benchmark: https://www.dominos.com/
