## Problems found
- The site is a stock "demo-restaurant" theme; the gallery files are still the theme's demo images (img/demos/restaurant/gallery/restaurant-gallery-1..5.jpg)
- The listed email is info@melodypicture.com, an address left over from another business
- The Instagram and Twitter icons link to the bare instagram.com and twitter.com homepages, not to the shop
- The "View More" buttons for Pasta, Dinners and Pizza & Salads are malformed links with no destination; the menu only lives in a third-party ordering pop-up
- The homepage copy is vague filler ("Feed a hungry crowd with this robust dinner menu made easy by tackling most of the preparation in advance") with a typo ("costumers")
- A "Latest Tweets" widget just says "Please wait..."
- Only two pages (Home, Contact Us) and no readable menu, hours only on the contact page
## Why it's a good opportunity
A cheap template that was never finished: wrong email, dead social buttons and placeholder copy make it an easy, obvious upgrade.
