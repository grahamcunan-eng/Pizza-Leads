- Leftover filler copy that doesn't fit a pizza shop, such as "Feed a hungry crowd with these robust sandwiches menu made easy by tackling most of the preparation in advance."
- Character-encoding glitch on the homepage ("weâ€™re" instead of "we're").
- Several links on the homepage go nowhere (href="#"), and images use stock template file names (images/portfolio/53.jpg, images/team/6.jpg).

## Why it's a good opportunity
Cheap, quickly made template site with copy that wasn't written for this shop. A custom site with real photos and local voice would stand out right away.

## Design notes
Keep the 24SevenEats ordering link as the main call to action, rewrite copy around the shop's long local history, fix encoding, and replace stock imagery.
