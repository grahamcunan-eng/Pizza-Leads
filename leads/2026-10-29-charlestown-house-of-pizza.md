# Charlestown House of Pizza (Charlestown, NH) - 8/10
- Website: https://charlestownnhpizza.com/
- Location: 203 Main St, Charlestown, NH 03603
- Phone: 603-826-3700
- Rating: 8/10
## Problems found
- Cheap hand-built Bootstrap/PHP site that looks quickly made, with a flat banner and a homepage that is mostly a grid of identical "Order Online" cards
- The Specials & Coupons page is still filled with stock template placeholders that have nothing to do with the shop: "Pizza With Ham, Rucola, And Vegetable - Upto 3.50% Cash Back", "Big Burger With French Fries Isolated", and "Delicious Turkish Pide Pizza - Upto 33.50% Off"
- The "Rewards" nav item goes to an empty "Coming Soon" page
- The "Job Application" link points to the Google Form's editor URL (/edit) instead of the public form link
- All ordering is handed off to a separate weborder.net page and DoorDash
## Why it's a good opportunity
A small family-owned shop with a quickly made site and fake placeholder specials, so the owner will see clear value in an upgrade: a parallax hero, a real specials section, and a cleaner, more visual menu built from their existing menu content.
## Design notes
Use the shop's existing content only. Never use AI-generated food images. Benchmark: https://www.dominos.com/
