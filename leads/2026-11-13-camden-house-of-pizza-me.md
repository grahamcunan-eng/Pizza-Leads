## Problems found
- The menu page is a single phone photo of the paper menu (IMG_3371.jpg), so it can't be read easily, searched, or zoomed well on a phone.
- Built on a stock WordPress default theme; the footer still shows default placeholder items (Blog, About, FAQs, Authors, Events) and "Twenty Twenty-Five" appears in the page text.
- Site icon is a cropped 2019 screenshot file ("screen-shot-2019-08-06...").
- The homepage slideshow says "This slideshow requires JavaScript," and the page has only two images.
- A public review notes the customer "tried ordering online but the website would not cooperate."

## Why it's a good opportunity
Popular coastal tourist-town shop (about 4.5 stars from roughly 490 reviews in a public listing) with a do-it-yourself site and a photographed paper menu. Clear before-and-after story.

## Design notes
Text-based menu with prices, a prominent Order Online button, real food and storefront photos, and directions, since reviewers say it's hard to find on a side street.
