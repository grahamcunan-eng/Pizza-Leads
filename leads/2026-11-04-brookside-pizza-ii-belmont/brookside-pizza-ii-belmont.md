## The shop's own words
- About (site): "Born and raised in Brooklyn N.Y. Christine relocated to N.H. in 1984. While living in the Loudon/Canterbury area she and her husband Frank met Billy and Sandy when they opened the original Brookside Pizza. In 1999 Christine joined forces with the Lazos family and began to learn the business. One year later Christine relocated to the Belmont location... In 2003, when Christine was offered partnership in the business she jumped at the opportunity... Now most days you will find her boys Michael and David and the rest of our crew hustling out the good food that Brookside has become known for. In April 2006 Brookside Pizza II opened 'The Tap Public House'... January 2009 our business was forced to shut down for a period of time do to a break in the sprinklers water main... we were able to reopen our business in May 2009... Our utmost appreciation goes out to our customers, friends and crew members. It is because of all of you that our business continues to succeed."

## Current site problems
- Squarespace site built ~2016–2017 under the bar's corporate name ("The Tap Public House Inc."); the pizza shop has no identity of its own on the web.
- The "Brookside Pizza II Menu" page (URL /contact-1) contains no menu at all — just the address block. The "Tap Public House Menu" page shows only leftover block labels ("menu-public", "New Gallery").
- The only real menu is two crooked phone photos of the paper menu, plus 2017 catering menu scans with "to Upload" in the file names.
- Wrong hours: the sidebar on every page says the business is open 11AM–12AM daily, while the location page and paper menu give earlier restaurant closing times.
- No online ordering; three different phone numbers with no clear "call to order" button.
- About text has typos ("do to a break", "re-coupe", "on sight bartending") and reads like a 2009 update.
- Merch and bar photos (shirts, jerseys, crowds) dominate; little about the pizza.

## Rating
7/10 — Family pizza shop with real history hidden inside a dated bar website: no menu page, only phone photos of the paper menu, wrong hours.
