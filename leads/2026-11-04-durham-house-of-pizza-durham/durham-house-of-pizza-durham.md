## Current site problems
- Same Wix restaurant template as other quickly made shop sites: footer "© 2023 by The Pizza Shop. Proudly created with Wix.com".
- Homepage is mostly template headings ("A PASSION FOR PIZZA", "Reservations", "Gallery", "FANCY A PIZZA PARTY?") with one short paragraph; nothing about the 1976 history, UNH, or late-night hours.
- "Join Our Team" page is completely empty (no text, no form).
- A separate "Unofficial Website" (dhoppizza.com) shows a different address (42 Main St) and different hours (open Sundays) — confusing for customers and search.
- Menu errors: Chicken Cacciatore lists a '10" Super' (should be 20"), "Cheesey Breatsticks"; size labels inconsistent across items.
- Hours formatting broken ("9 :00P M"); no map or photo of the storefront on the homepage.
- No social links anywhere on the site.

## Rating
8/10 — Long-running college-town shop (est. 1976) on an unedited Wix template with an empty jobs page and a confusing unofficial site competing with it.
