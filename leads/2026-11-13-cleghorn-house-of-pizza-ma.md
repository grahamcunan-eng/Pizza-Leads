## Problems found
- Homepage still shows an out-of-date notice: "We will be closed Aug. 16 - Sep 16. Reopening Sep 17, 2025."
- No online ordering anywhere on the site; customers have to call.
- Homepage is very thin: a two-sentence welcome, the address and phone numbers.
- Menu is a long text page plus a printable PDF, with no food photos.

## Why it's a good opportunity
30+ year shop with a dated, small agency site that nobody is keeping current. A stale closure banner more than a year old is an easy, concrete talking point.

## Design notes
Remove the stale notice, add online ordering, turn the menu into a mobile-friendly categorized layout, and add photos and a short history section.
