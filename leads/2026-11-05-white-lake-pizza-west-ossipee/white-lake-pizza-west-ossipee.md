## Current site problems
- 2018 Squarespace template (assets dated March 2018); homepage is a logo, two one-line banners and a wall of hours and payment rules, with no photos of the shop or food.
- Menu is split across seven separate nav pages (Appetizers, Pizzas, Calzones, Subs, Dinners, Salads, Kids Meal), with no single menu view; layout prints every price twice around each item name.
- "careers" page (linked from "now hiring!") is empty: it shows only the tax disclaimer, no job info or application.
- Summer and off-season hours both shown, so visitors have to work out which applies.
- Ordering lives on a separate VersiEats page; the site banner itself isn't a clear button.
- Price oddities that read like surcharged figures ($13.78, $16.38, $2.08 toppings); specialty pizza and calzone prices don't line up.
- No about section, no Facebook or Instagram links, "Powered by Squarespace" footer.

## Rating
