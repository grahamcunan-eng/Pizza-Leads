## Problems found
- A one-page vendor template (footer credit to eztouse.com, uploads dated 2018) with a footer that still says "©2022"
- The header "Order Now!" is just the phone number. There is no online ordering at all
- The menu is a long text-only price list on the homepage with no photos next to items
- Generic SEO filler copy ("Pizza & More For Take-Out & Delivery in farmington, ME", with a lowercase town name)
- Every image has an empty alt attribute
- The only real nav item is "home"
## Why it's a good opportunity
A family pizzeria open since 1988 that still takes orders by phone only from a thin 2018 template, so a modern site with online ordering and a proper menu would make a big difference.
