## Problems found
- Site-builder template with leftovers: the header still shows the placeholder contact details "555-555-5555" and "mymail@mailservice.com" on every page
- The Menu page still has the template's placeholder slider text ("Breathtaking colors of our planet", "Faces of Humanity", "Sands of Time", "Beyond Boundaries") and a row of unlabeled "Button" buttons
- Instagram and Yelp icons point to the generic instagram.com and yelp.com/biz/ pages instead of the shop's profiles
- A WhatsApp link is wired to the shop's landline number
- No online ordering; typo-like "L ike and Follow us" text in the footer; the same address block repeats three times in the header area
## Why it's a good opportunity
A family-owned shop under new management since 2023 that is actively upgrading the store, with a quickly made site full of visible placeholders, so an upgrade with a parallax hero and a cleaner, more visual menu is an easy sell.
