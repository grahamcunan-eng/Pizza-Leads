## Problems found
- A one-page vendor template (footer credit to eztouse.com) with a footer that still says "© 2022"
- The only nav items are Home and Privacy Notice; catering, banquet rooms and menu have no pages of their own
- Confusing history: the page title says "Best Pizza Since 2002" while the copy says the shop has been around "Since 1998"
- Ordering is split between a Slice link (an icon with no text label) and a Google Play link to a ChowNow app, with no single clear Order button
- Every image has an empty alt attribute
- Menu typo: daily specials "Served with Fries of O-Rings" (should be "or")
## Why it's a good opportunity
A long-running family pizza and Greek restaurant with banquet and catering services buried in a thin 2022 template, so a clearer site with one ordering path would be a solid upgrade.
