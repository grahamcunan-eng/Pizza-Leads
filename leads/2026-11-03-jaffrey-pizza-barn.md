# Jaffrey Pizza Barn (Jaffrey, NH) - 8/10
- Website: https://jaffreypizzabarn.com/
- Location: 6 Blake Street, Jaffrey, NH 03452
- Phone: (603) 532-8383
- Rating: 8/10
## Problems found
- Old Community Communications template; the whole homepage is one short paragraph plus two images, telling visitors almost nothing (no hours, specials or menu highlights)
- The site's own menu page (/menu.html) is empty: just the header and footer, no menu content at all
- The "Menu" nav item skips the site entirely and sends visitors to a third-party eDiningExpress ordering page, the same link as "Online Ordering"
- "Order Online" and "Delivery Available" buttons are flat PNG images rather than real buttons
- Viewport tag uses old, invalid syntax with user-scalable=0, which blocks pinch-zoom on phones
- Hours and contact info are split onto separate thin pages (hours.html, contact.html) instead of being on the homepage
- Dated, plain visuals compared with dominos.com
## Why it's a good opportunity
A family shop in business since 1973 whose site is a bare, quickly made vendor template with an empty menu page, so a clean, modern rebuild built from its existing content is an obvious upgrade.
## Design notes
Use the shop's existing content only. Never use AI-generated food images. Benchmark: https://www.dominos.com/
