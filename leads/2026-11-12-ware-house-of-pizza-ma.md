## Problems found
- A stock Menufy ordering template; the only image on the homepage is the logo
- Vague, generic homepage copy ("Delicious Slices of Happiness!", "your newest dining destination") with no story about the shop
- A "Contact Form" heading appears on the homepage, but there is no form in the page's HTML
- Boilerplate listing fields shown as content ("Atmosphere: Chill", "Service Options: Free Parking")
- The same hours are repeated three times (business, carryout and delivery)
- A second domain, warepizza.com, still shows up in search results with the shop's name and address but now opens a blank web-host default page
- No social links on the site
## Why it's a good opportunity
A newer independent shop running on a bare ordering-platform template with a vague homepage and a dead second domain, so a real branded site would stand out right away.
## Design notes
Use the shop's existing content only. Never use AI-generated food images. Benchmark: https://www.dominos.com/
