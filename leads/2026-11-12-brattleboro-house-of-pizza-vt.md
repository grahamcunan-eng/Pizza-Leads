## Problems found
- A single-page WordPress site on an old "Tomato" restaurant theme (Spyropress), with only a short About blurb, three testimonials, hours and contact
- The only menu is a 4.7 MB PDF linked from the nav, so there is no readable or searchable menu on the site
- Footer still says "Copyright ® 2018" (with a registered-trademark sign instead of ©)
- Site images still load over plain http:// and several have generic or missing alt text ("logo" repeated, an image file named "Unknown-300x35.png")
- The copy tells people to "download the DoorDash app for delivery" while the nav's Order Online button goes to a separate order.online page
- Only three photos on the whole site, all small 2021 uploads
## Why it's a good opportunity
A family shop serving Brattleboro since 1985 whose site is a dated one-page theme with a PDF-only menu, so a clean site with a real menu and clear ordering would be an easy upgrade.
