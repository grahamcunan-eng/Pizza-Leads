## The shop's own words
- "Welcome to Mary's Pizza — Join us for great home-style cooking!"
- Awards: "Voted Best Pizzeria of the Great North Woods Region — New Hampshire Magazine 2018"; "Voted Best Pizza in NH 2016 & 2018 — WMUR 9's 2016 & 2018 Viewers Choice"; menu: "Voted Best Pizza In New Hampshire By Viewers Of WMUR Channel 9 News"; "Featured In Viva Italia Magazine & New Hampshire To Do Magazine"
- History (About page and menu): "Celebrating over seventy plus years, Mary's Pizza has been an established staple of the North Country, proudly serving a variety of homemade pasta dishes & fresh dough pizzas. What started as a vision back in 1947 by Mary herself & husband Camillo Ferrante, has now, even to this day, been a huge favorite amongst our customers, near & far."
- "We have been featured in both "NH To Do" and "Viva Italia" Magazines, and have been honored by WMUR - TV'S "Viewers Choice" Award of "Best Pizza" over the years, winning #1 twice and always consistently placing in the Top Five in our beautiful state of New Hampshire."
- "Mary's prides itself on using the best ingredients sourced from premium vendors, creating all dishes with ultimate quality and putting customer service above all."
- "Passed on through generations of hard work and dedication, from founders Camillo & Mary, to Tony & Lorraine, to Jim & Maggie, and now current owners, Phil & Thera, Mary's Pizza has thrived. So whether it's been your second time this week, back visiting for the holidays, or the tourist that finds us, your support and loyalty is what continues to bring this establishment such success."
- "We thank you immensely and look forward to serving you on your next visit. Many Thanks Today and Always, Mary's Pizza Familia"
- Crust note: "All pizzas can be ordered with your choice of crust: Thin crust (crispy), Regular crust (original), Pan (thick crust), Gluten-free (GF, available in small only); Thin or regular cooked on a screen (soft center crust); Roman Pizza (thin crispy pizza, characterized by its low, crunchy crust and generous toppings)"

## Current site problems
- GoDaddy Website Builder 8 site, footer still "Copyright © 2018".
- The online-ordering link in the footer is a raw, mangled Proofpoint "urldefense" email-scanner URL printed as text, clearly pasted from an email.
- Menu is only downloadable PDFs (made in Illustrator, so the text isn't searchable or readable on a phone without zooming); the "Menu" nav item just opens the download page.
- Homepage is thin: name repeated as headings, a short "Join us for great home-style cooking!" line, an 8-slide carousel and the awards.
- Mobile header repeats "Mary's Pizza" several times (layout duplicates).
- No link to Facebook or Instagram; no delivery or catering info.
- A 79-year-old, award-winning family story buried on a plain About page.
