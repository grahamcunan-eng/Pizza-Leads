# Millo's Pizza & Grill (Barrington, NH) - 8/10
- Website: https://millospizza.com/
- Location: 575 Franklin Pierce Highway, Barrington, NH 03825
- Phone: (603) 664-8871
- Rating: 8/10
## Problems found
- GoDaddy Website Builder site; the homepage is nearly empty: a "View Our Menu" button, a "A Few Of Our Dishes" section that shows just one item (Pepperoni Pizza), then the address and hours
- Homepage says nothing about the shop itself (no story, no specials, no reason to choose them)
- Menu is a long plain-text price list with no layout or sections that stand out
- Typos on the menu ("cucumberes", "Caeser")
- No online ordering anywhere on the site; phone only, even though they offer delivery
- Specials page is a short bare text list with no dates or details
- Generic builder look with poor visuals compared with dominos.com
## Why it's a good opportunity
A single-location local shop with a cheap, quickly made builder site and an almost empty homepage, so the owner would clearly see the value of a polished, easy-to-navigate site built from their existing menu.
## Design notes
Use the shop's existing content only. Never use AI-generated food images. Benchmark: https://www.dominos.com/
