# Leominster House of Pizza — Leominster, MA

## Basics
- **Website:** https://leominsterhouseofpizza.com/ (FoodTec ordering site) plus a second site at https://leominsterhouseofpizza.godaddysites.com/
- **Address:** 753 Central Street, Leominster, MA 01453
- **Phone:** (978) 534-4222
- **Facebook:** https://facebook.com/121303407885354
- **Instagram:** none found
- **Online ordering:** https://leominsterhouseofpizza.foodtecsolutions.com/ordering/intro
- **Rating:** 9/10

## Problems found
- The shop's web presence is split across two sites: a stock FoodTec ordering template on its own domain and a separate free GoDaddy subdomain site.
- The GoDaddy homepage is a single line ("Welcome to Leominster House of pizza! Join us for a meal to remember!") with one image and GoDaddy's default app logo.
- The own-domain site lists the address as just "Central St" with no street number, and its main pitch is a FoodTec "Register" signup box.
- Neither site has an about story, photos of the food, or social links.

## Why it's a good opportunity
A busy town shop with real online ordering but no real website: two thin, mismatched pages that look unfinished. One clean branded site on its own domain would be an obvious upgrade.

## Design notes
Merge into one site on leominsterhouseofpizza.com with full address, hours, a readable menu, a prominent Order Online button to the existing FoodTec link, and the Facebook page.
