# Uncle Joey's (Merrimack, NH) - 8/10
- Website: https://www.unclejoeysnh.com/
- Location: 733 Daniel Webster Hwy, Merrimack, NH (a second location in Nashua has its own site)
- Phone: (603) 424-5693
- Rating: 8/10
## Problems found
- Cheap single-page Wix site: the hero title "Uncle Joey's of Merrimack" is overlapped by the tagline text and a popup that covers the page on load
- Menu is just a single image of the printed menu (plus a catering menu image), so it can't be read on a phone, searched, or clicked
- Navigation has an empty first button, and the remaining nav items only scroll the same page
- The "Order Online" button links to a Slice URL carrying copied Google Ads tracking codes (utm_source=PaidGoogle, gclid=...)
- Hours block ends with a stray "Closed" line that doesn't name a day
## Why it's a good opportunity
A small family-run shop (two locations) with a quickly made site and an image-only menu. A parallax hero and a well-designed, readable menu built from their existing content would be a clear upgrade.
## Design notes
Use the shop's existing content only. Never use AI-generated food images. Benchmark: https://www.dominos.com/
