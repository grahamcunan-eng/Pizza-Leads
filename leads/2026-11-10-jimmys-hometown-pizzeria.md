## Problems found
- The homepage is just "A Slice of Home", the address, phone numbers and hours; no description of the shop or food
- The Menu page has no text at all, only images, so it can't be searched or read easily on a phone
- The Delivery page has no content beyond the shared header and footer
- The only real store is a merch shop (cap, tee, hoodie); there is no online food ordering
- A scrolling hours banner repeats the same line four times
## Why it's a good opportunity
A bare Squarespace shell with an image-only menu and no food ordering, so a real menu and ordering site would be a clear step up.
