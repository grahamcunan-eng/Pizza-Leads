## Problems found
- The whole site is one WordPress page with a single 2016 header image and no food photos
- The full menu is one long text price list
- The menu links point to a /menu/ page that just redirects back to the homepage
- Delivery is only by calling or emailing a separate service, SweenEats
- There is no online ordering
- There are no social links on the site
## Why it's a good opportunity
A shop open since 1974 on a one-page, text-only site with no photos and no ordering, so a clean rebuild is an obvious, easy-to-show upgrade.

Use the shop's existing content only. Never use AI-generated food images. Benchmark: https://www.dominos.com/
