## 5. Current site problems

1. It's a dated "Web-Kare" template (copyright 2023, but the code is old). It loads html5shiv from Google Code, which now returns 404, and still ships IE7 stylesheets.
2. The viewport tag uses `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=no`, which blocks pinch-zoom on phones.
3. Every menu page is a plain text price table with no photos or descriptions for most items. The only images are four small generic stock photos (pasta, pizza slice, fries, meatball sub) plus a Coca-Cola logo.
4. The homepage is vague: a wall of seasonal hours, a delivery line and four "View Menu" buttons. It says nothing about the shop, Teddy's Lounge or the owners.
5. "DAILY LUNCH SPECIALS" is advertised but no specials are listed. The "Super Pizza Specials" and gluten-free blocks are commented out of the code.
6. The catering page lists every item as "$CALL". Its "Print Menu" PDF is a Word doc dated **3/2014** with old hours (it says open Sundays).
7. Menu typos and inconsistencies: "Glunten Free", "Mozarella Sticks", "Rueben", "Potatos", "turket", "mossarella". Gluten-free pizza has two different prices ($12.50 vs $16.95). The add-on price on the Parmesan calzones is cut off ("Large $3.").
8. There are no links to Facebook (the page is active, with 511 followers) and no Instagram. A fax number is shown as a main contact method.
9. Teddy's Lounge (the bar) has no page, hours or description, though their Facebook promotes it (football nights, new bartender, "stop by our lounge for dinner").
10. Ordering goes to a separate e-tab app with different branding.

**Rating: 8/10.** Reason: a downtown pizza shop and lounge stuck on an old template site with text-only menus, a 2014 catering PDF, no specials shown and no social links.
