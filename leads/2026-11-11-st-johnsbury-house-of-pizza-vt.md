## Problems found
- A single-page Wix site. The only pages are the homepage and an unused Wix "members-area" page
- The browser tab/page title is cut off as "HOME | St Johnsbury House o", and it is also used as the share (og) title
- The menu is only a few photo images ("Click the picture to view the full photo"), with no text menu, prices or search-friendly content
- There is no online ordering and no Order or Call button, just two phone numbers in the Contact block
- The hero image is a Wix library image, not an uploaded shop photo, and no images have alt text
- The page says almost nothing about the shop's family history since 1979, beyond one sentence
## Why it's a good opportunity
A family pizzeria in business since 1979 that runs on a bare one-page Wix site with a truncated title and an image-only menu, which a proper menu and ordering site would clearly improve.
