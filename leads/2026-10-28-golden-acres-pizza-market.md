- Website: https://pizzamarketgoffstown.com/
- Location: 670 Mast Road, Goffstown, NH
- Phone: (603) 623-0899
- Rating: 9/10
## Problems found
- Menu page has no real menu: it shows raw, unrendered page-builder code ("[ux_banner height=...]", "[button text="Order now"...]", "[ux_gallery ids=...]") in place of content and buttons
- Navigation still has WordPress theme leftovers: a "#75 (no title)" link plus "Style" and "Uncategorized" category links that lead to an empty "Hello world!" sample post
- Header has a strip of tiny, unreadable links, and the phone number is shown unformatted as "6036230899"
- Homepage is a flat red page with a cramped block of small text, and the "Family-owned and operated for 24 years" line is out of date (they opened in 1998)
- Footer still says "Fast Food WordPress Theme By Buywptemplate"
## Why it's a good opportunity
A family-owned shop (Sam and Sharon Galatas, since 1998) with a cheap, broken template site. A clean parallax homepage and a real, readable menu built from their existing content would be an obvious upgrade.
## Design notes
Use the shop's existing content only. Never use AI-generated food images. Benchmark: https://www.dominos.com/
