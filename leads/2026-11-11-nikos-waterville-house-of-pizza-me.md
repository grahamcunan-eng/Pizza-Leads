## Problems found
- The homepage is generic SEO copy ("Calling all college students and pizza lovers!", "Did Someone Say Fresh Pizza?") with no real shop story, photos of the food or hours
- The Menu page has no menu on it, just a bullet list of categories (Wraps, Salads, Steaks...) and a "View Menu" button
- That button opens a PDF dated January 2023 with a messy file name ("..._updated_1-26-23_compressed_page-0001_(1)-2-3_CORRECT.pdf")
- The Order Now link (watervillepizza.foodtecsolutions.com) failed to connect on three tries from our test box with a TLS error, while other FoodTec stores loaded fine. Recheck this before outreach
- The Contact page says "Fill out the form on this page to order", but there is no form in the page's HTML
- Typo "Niko Waterville House of Pizza" on the homepage. The site says "over 45 years" but the shop's Facebook says "30+ years"
- The viewport is set to user-scalable=no, which blocks pinch-zoom on phones
- No social links anywhere on the site
## Why it's a good opportunity
A Colby College-area family shop whose site is agency filler copy with no real menu and a possibly broken order button, so a clean rebuild with a readable menu would be an obvious upgrade.
