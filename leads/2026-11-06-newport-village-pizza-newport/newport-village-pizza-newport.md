## Current site problems
- Multiple conflicting hours blocks on the same live pages (Tue/Wed open vs closed; Sunday open vs closed; start times 10:30 vs 11:00; one banner string shows “12 am” for Sunday).
- WordPress menu HTML duplicates many item blocks (same appetizer/pizza entries appear twice).
- Online ordering is third-party DoorDash order.online branded “Village Pizza of Newport,” not a first-party cart.
- Two phone numbers printed without on-page explanation of which line to use.
- Instagram: none found (Facebook only).
- Minor copy/filename typos exist (`ton’s`, `villiage`, `spagehti`) — noted but not the basis for the rating.

## Rating
- 8/10 — Real independent Newport shop with a working site, full priced menu, and photos, but the live site publishes incompatible hours schedules and duplicated menu markup that a redesign would fix.
