# Mickey's N.Y. Pizza (Hudson & Derry, NH) - 9/10
- Website: https://www.mickeysnypizza.com/
- Location: Hudson, NH (also Derry, NH)
- Phone: Hudson (603) 883-0444, Derry (603) 552-3503
- Rating: 9/10
## Problems found
- Homepage is just one banner image and an Order Now button, with nothing about the shop, hours, or address
- Menu is a basic text-only layout that could be made far more visually appealing
- Footer says "© Crush Pizza" (another business's name)
- "Join Our Team" opens a "Job Application Coming Soon" page that still has Wix sample text; no way to apply online
## Why it's a good opportunity
A small local shop with a cheap, quickly made site. A parallax hero image, a well-designed menu, and an online job application form built from their existing content would be an obvious upgrade.
## Design notes
Use the shop's existing content only. Never use AI-generated food images. Benchmark: https://www.dominos.com/
