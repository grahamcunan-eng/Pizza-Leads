## Problems found
- A Ziplocal/Divi template ("Powered by Ziplocal", "Designed by Elegant Themes") with only three pages
- The homepage is one paragraph and a Menu button, and the only images are the logo and one 2017 cover photo
- The header "Order Now" is plain text next to the phone number. There is no ordering, only calling
- The menu is a long text-only price list with no photos
- Page titles are keyword-stuffed and don't match the pages ("Cheese Calzones in Morrisville, VT" for the Menu page, "Homemade Pizza Dough in Morrisville, VT" for Contact Us)
- Hours (Tue-Sat 11-8) are only on the Contact page, and the address wording differs between the footer and the Contact page
- No social links
## Why it's a good opportunity
A family-owned shop of about 30 years on a thin directory-vendor template with no photos or ordering, which a simple modern site would clearly improve.
