## Problems found
- Stock WordPress "Flash" theme (ThemeGrill credit in the footer), with a homepage of stacked text blocks
- The "Breakfast Menu" page is empty: just the heading plus the store hours footer
- The "Vegan Sandwiches" nav item opens a page whose URL is /weekly-specials/ (a repurposed page)
- Rough, broken English in the Our Story section ("Acquired Princeton house of pizza using victor experience from Italy", "Now Daughter's Mary and Sarah are Join the family team")
- The founding date conflicts across three places: "founded in 1960s" (home), "since 2009" (Employment page), "since 1991" (Facebook)
- Menu typos and inconsistencies: "IMPOSSIBILE Vegan Checken", "Brocolli"; gourmet pizza prices have no $ signs; pizza sizes are listed as 10"/15" but the thin-crust upcharge says 16"
- The only online ordering link (Slice) sits on the empty Breakfast page. The homepage and Menu page have no Order button
- No social links on the site, though the shop is active on Facebook and TikTok
## Why it's a good opportunity
A long-running family shop whose cheap theme site has an empty page, sloppy copy and a hidden ordering link, all easy wins for a redesign.
